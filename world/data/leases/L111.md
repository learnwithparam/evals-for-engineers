# Commercial Lease L111

Fernhill Homes lease for unit G1 at Fernhill Arcade. Template: FH-COM-2018.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Delaney Holdings ("Landlord") and Corner Loaf Bakery ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit G1 at 89 Fernhill Arcade ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 September 2024 and ends on 31 August 2029.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $5,550.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

On each anniversary of the start date, the monthly rent shall increase by 3% over the rent then payable, rounded to the nearest cent.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $16,650.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by the 10th of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of 5% of the overdue amount.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Animals

No animals other than assistance animals are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may sublet the Premises with Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not withhold without good reason.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of five years by giving written notice not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant may end this lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee of $33,300.00.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
