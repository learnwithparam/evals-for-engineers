# Residential Lease L032

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 3F at Ivy Wharf. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Fischer Estates ("Landlord") and Liam Sato ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 3F at 4 Ivy Wharf ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 January 2025 and ends on 31 December 2026.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,375.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,375.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 3 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $75.00.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Pets

No pets are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice sixty (60) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant may end this lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee of $2,750.00.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.

## a01 Amendment No. 1

Signed by both parties. From 1 October 2025, clause c04 is changed: the monthly rent is $1,475.00. All other terms stay the same.
