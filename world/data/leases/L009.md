# Residential Lease L009

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 10D at Dunlin Court. Template: FH-RES-2019.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Sato Partners ("Landlord") and Maya Haddad ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 10D at 114 Dunlin Court ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 September 2025 and ends on 31 August 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,500.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

On each anniversary of the start date, the monthly rent shall increase by 3% over the rent then payable, rounded to the nearest cent.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,250.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 5 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $50.00.

## c08 Utilities

Landlord pays for gas and trash and water. Tenant pays for all other utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $35.00 per month.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice not less than thirty (30) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant may end this lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee of $3,000.00.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
