# Residential Lease L130

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 4E at Dunlin Court. Template: FH-RES-2026.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Sato Partners ("Landlord") and Cal Abara ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 4E at 114 Dunlin Court ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 September 2026 and ends on 31 August 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,225.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,450.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 3 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $75.00.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $50.00 per month and a one-time pet deposit of $300.00.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Extension

If the Tenant wants to stay on, the Tenant can extend for a further 12 months by telling Fernhill at least 60 days before the term ends.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
