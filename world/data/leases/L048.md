# Residential Lease L048

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 2F at Rowan Steps. Template: FH-RES-2019.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Quint Family Trust ("Landlord") and Zara Mensah ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 2F at 107 Rowan Steps ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 March 2025 and ends on 28 February 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,950.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,950.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 5 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $75.00.

## c08 Utilities

Landlord pays for trash. Tenant pays for all other utilities.

## c09 Pets

No pets are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may sublet the Premises with Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not withhold without good reason.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice not less than ninety (90) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant may end this lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee of $5,900.00.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.

## a01 Amendment No. 1

Signed by both parties. From 1 December 2025, clause c04 is changed: the monthly rent is $3,050.00. All other terms stay the same.
