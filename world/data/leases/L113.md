# Commercial Lease L113

Fernhill Homes lease for unit G4 at Clover Mill. Template: FH-COM-2018.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Varga Family Trust ("Landlord") and Greenleaf Pharmacy ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit G4 at 53 Clover Mill ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 May 2023 and ends on 30 April 2028.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $6,500.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

On each anniversary of the start date, the monthly rent shall increase by 3% over the rent then payable, rounded to the nearest cent.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $19,500.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 10 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of 5% of the overdue amount.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Animals

No animals other than assistance animals are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may sublet the Premises with Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not withhold without good reason.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of five years by giving written notice one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
