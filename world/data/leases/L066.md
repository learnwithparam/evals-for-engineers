# Residential Lease L066

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 2B at Medlar Place. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Nakamura Estates ("Landlord") and Yusuf Okafor ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 2B at 159 Medlar Place ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 December 2024 and ends on 30 November 2026.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,150.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The monthly rent changes as follows.

| From | Monthly rent |
|---|---|
| 1 December 2025 | $2,193.00 |

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,150.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 5 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $75.00.

## c08 Utilities

Landlord pays for trash and water. Tenant pays for all other utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $25.00 per month and a one-time pet deposit of $300.00.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice sixty (60) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant may end this lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee of $4,300.00.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
