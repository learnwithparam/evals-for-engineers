# Residential Lease L099

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 5D at Medlar Place. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Nakamura Estates ("Landlord") and Ade Osei ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 5D at 159 Medlar Place ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 October 2025 and ends on 30 September 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,225.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

On each anniversary of the start date, the monthly rent shall increase by 4% over the rent then payable, rounded to the nearest cent.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,450.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by the 3rd of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $50.00.

## c08 Utilities

Landlord pays for water. Tenant pays for all other utilities.

## c09 Pets

No pets are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

This lease has no renewal option.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
