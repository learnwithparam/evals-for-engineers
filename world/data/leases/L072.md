# Residential Lease L072

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 2B at Granary Wharf. Template: FH-RES-2019.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Shah Partners ("Landlord") and Kemi Delaney ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 2B at 126 Granary Wharf ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 April 2025 and ends on 31 March 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $3,050.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $3,050.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 5 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $100.00.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $25.00 per month and a one-time pet deposit of $300.00.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice not less than thirty (30) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.

## a01 Amendment No. 1

Signed by both parties. From 1 August 2025, clause c04 is changed: the monthly rent is $3,200.00. All other terms stay the same.
