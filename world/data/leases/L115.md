# Commercial Lease L115

Fernhill Homes lease for unit G3 at Fernhill Arcade. Template: FH-COM-2018.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Delaney Holdings ("Landlord") and Brightside Opticians ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit G3 at 89 Fernhill Arcade ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 June 2022 and ends on 31 May 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $10,200.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $30,600.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 10 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of 5% of the overdue amount.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Animals

No animals other than assistance animals are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may sublet the Premises with Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not withhold without good reason.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of five years by giving written notice not less than one hundred and twenty (120) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.

## a01 Amendment No. 1

Signed by both parties. From 1 April 2023, clause c04 is changed: the monthly rent is $10,710.00. Clause c05 no longer applies. All other terms stay the same.
