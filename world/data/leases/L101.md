# Residential Lease L101

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 5A at Vetch Square. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Fischer Estates ("Landlord") and Isla Mensah ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 5A at 96 Vetch Square ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 December 2024 and ends on 30 November 2026.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,025.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $3,038.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by the 3rd of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $50.00.

## c08 Utilities

Landlord pays for water. Tenant pays for all other utilities.

## c09 Pets

No pets are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice not less than ninety (90) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
