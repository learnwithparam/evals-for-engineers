# Residential Lease L010

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 6F at Elm Terrace. Template: FH-RES-2019.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Shah Estates ("Landlord") and Uma Moreau ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 6F at 7 Elm Terrace ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 November 2025 and ends on 31 October 2026.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $1,800.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The rent does not change during the term.

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $1,800.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 3 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $50.00.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $35.00 per month.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may sublet the Premises with Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not withhold without good reason.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice ninety (90) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant may end this lease early by giving 60 days' written notice and paying an early termination fee of $3,600.00.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
