# Residential Lease L015

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 6B at Alder Row. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Pereira Partners ("Landlord") and Chloe Okafor ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 6B at 173 Alder Row ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 October 2025 and ends on 30 September 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,050.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The monthly rent changes as follows.

| From | Monthly rent |
|---|---|
| 1 October 2026 | $2,111.50 |

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,050.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by day 3 of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $100.00.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $50.00 per month and a one-time pet deposit of $300.00.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice sixty (60) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
