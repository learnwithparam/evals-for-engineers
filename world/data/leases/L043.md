# Residential Lease L043

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 3E at Kestrel Way. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Fischer Family Trust ("Landlord") and Ewa Mensah ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 3E at 45 Kestrel Way ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 October 2025 and ends on 30 September 2027.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $3,375.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The monthly rent changes as follows.

| From | Monthly rent |
|---|---|
| 1 October 2026 | $3,476.25 |

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $5,062.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by the 5th of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $50.00.

## c08 Utilities

Landlord pays for trash. Tenant pays for all other utilities.

## c09 Pets

Tenant may keep up to two pets. Tenant shall pay a pet fee of $50.00 per month and a one-time pet deposit of $300.00.

## c10 Parking

One parking space is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may sublet the Premises with Landlord's written consent, which Landlord will not withhold without good reason.

## c12 Renewal

Tenant may renew this lease for one further term of 12 months by giving written notice thirty (30) days before the end of the term.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
