# Residential Lease L007

Fernhill Homes lease for unit 4F at Vetch Square. Template: FH-RES-2022.

## c01 Parties

This lease is between Fernhill Homes, as managing agent for Fischer Estates ("Landlord") and Priya Novak ("Tenant").

## c02 Premises

Landlord lets to Tenant unit 4F at 96 Vetch Square ("the Premises").

## c03 Term

The term starts on 1 November 2024 and ends on 31 October 2026.

## c04 Rent

Tenant shall pay monthly rent of $2,450.00, due on the first day of each month.

## c05 Rent increases

The monthly rent changes as follows.

| From | Monthly rent |
|---|---|
| 1 November 2025 | $2,548.00 |

## c06 Security deposit

Tenant has paid a security deposit of $2,450.00, held under the deposit rules that apply to the Premises.

## c07 Late charges

If rent is not received by the 3rd of the month, Tenant shall pay a late charge of $50.00.

## c08 Utilities

Tenant pays for all utilities.

## c09 Pets

No pets are allowed on the Premises.

## c10 Parking

No parking is included.

## c11 Assignment and subletting

Tenant may not assign this lease or sublet any part of the Premises.

## c12 Renewal

This lease has no renewal option.

## c13 Early termination

Tenant has no right to end this lease early.

## c14 Notices

Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in this lease. A posted notice is treated as received 3 days after posting.
